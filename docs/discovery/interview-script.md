# Reader interview script (30 minutes)

**Goal:** learn how readers find people to read with, what spoils books for them, and what organisers struggle with. We're collecting **specific past behaviour**, not opinions about a future product.

**Who:** fantasy, romantasy and romance readers; book-club organisers (at least one per week).

**Golden rules**
- Ask about the last time it happened, not "would you…?".
- Don't pitch GawainBook until the last 5 minutes.
- Silence is fine. Count to five before filling it.
- When something sounds important, ask "Tell me more" or "What happened next?".
- Write down their words, not your interpretation.

---

## Before the call (2 min)

- [ ] Confirm the time zone and the call link.
- [ ] Open a copy of [`notes-template.md`](notes-template.md) as `YYYY-MM-DD-<initials>.md`. Never use full names.

## Opening (3 min)

> Thanks for making time. I'm building something for readers and I want to understand how you read and talk about books today. There are no right answers, and nothing you say can hurt my feelings.
>
> It'll take about 30 minutes. **Is it OK if I take notes?** *(If recording: Is it OK if I record the audio, only for my own notes? I'll delete it within 30 days.)*
>
> You can skip any question or stop at any time.

Only record if they say yes. Write down that they agreed, and when.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. What are you reading right now? How did you pick it?
2. Roughly how many books do you finish in a month?

## Core questions (17 min)

3. **Tell me about the last book you discussed with someone.** Where did that happen: in person, in a group chat, online?
   - Who was it with? How did you find them?
   - What made that conversation good, or not?
4. **How do you find people to read with?** What's frustrating about it?
   - What did you try last time? What happened?
5. **Tell me about the last time a book was spoiled for you.** What happened next?
   - Where was it: social media, a review, a friend?
   - What did you do afterwards? Do you avoid anything now because of it?
6. **What tools do you use today** for reading with others or tracking books (Goodreads, StoryGraph, Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram…)? What do you wish they did?
7. *(Organisers only)* **Walk me through how you ran your last session.**
   - What took the most time: picking the book, scheduling, reminding people, writing questions, keeping the conversation going?
   - How many people signed up, and how many actually showed up?
   - What made people drop out?

## Looking ahead (3 min)

8. **If a group like this existed, what would make you come back every week?**
9. What would make you leave?

## Pitch and wrap-up (5 min)

> Here's what I'm building: small reading circles where everyone reads the same book at their own pace, and chapter discussions stay locked until you reach that chapter, so nobody gets spoiled.

- What's your first reaction? What's confusing?
- Would you try it with an existing group? Who would you bring?
- **Can I email you when the beta is ready?** If yes, send them the waitlist link: https://gawainbook.onrender.com/waitlist
- Is there anyone else I should talk to?

> Thank you, this was really helpful.

## After the call (10 min)

- [ ] Finish the notes the same day. Mark the three most surprising quotes.
- [ ] Add one line to [`patterns.md`](patterns.md) for each pattern you heard.
- [ ] Delete any recording within 30 days.
