# Recruiting post

Use these to book interviews. Before posting in any community, **read its self-promotion rules**, and take part in the conversation first. Many book communities only allow research requests in a weekly thread, or not at all.

Replace `<your contact>` with how people should reach you (an email address or "DM me").

## Main version (English)

> **Looking for readers to chat with for 30 minutes 📚**
>
> I'm a developer building a small project for people who love reading with others: book clubs, buddy reads and readalongs, especially in **fantasy, romantasy and romance**. Before I build more, I want to learn how you actually find reading buddies, run your book club, and avoid spoilers.
>
> It's a relaxed 30-minute video or voice call. No sales pitch and nothing to buy. I'll just ask about your reading habits. If you organise a book club or buddy reads, I'd especially love to hear from you.
>
> Interested? Reply here or contact me at `<your contact>`. Thank you!

## Short version (Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, X)

> Do you read with a book club or buddy reads? 📚 I'm building something for fantasy, romantasy and romance readers and would love 30 minutes of your time to hear how you read with others today. No pitch, just questions. DM me 💛

## Book-club organisers (direct message)

> Hi <name>! I saw that you run <club>. I'm a developer working on tools for reading groups, and I'd love to learn how you organise your sessions: what works, what takes the most time, and what makes members drop out. Would you be up for a 30-minute call sometime next week? No sales pitch, just questions. Thank you either way!

## Where to look (check each place's rules first)

- University reading societies and literature student groups
- Local book clubs (Meetup, library and bookshop events)
- Discord servers for buddy reads and readalongs
- Small bookstagram and BookTok creators who run readalongs
- Reddit communities that allow research or feedback posts (often only in a pinned weekly thread)
